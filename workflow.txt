STUDY 3 GENOTYPE RNA TRIANGULATION
Author: Vusumuzi Mabasa
All participants, variants, genes, coordinates, LD and modules are synthetic.
No proteins, actual patient data or real biological enrichment are included.

OBJECTIVE
Evaluate complementary MOFA+ discovery, DIABLO supervised signatures and regional
cis-eQTL inference, then quantify convergence at gene, locus and synthetic-module
levels. Agreement is not independent biological validation or causal evidence.

DESIGN
Five scenarios: null, regulation_only, abundance_only, convergent, interaction_only.
Training N240/480, twenty pairs per cell, independent N360 test participants per pair.
Total200 independent training/test pairs. Two implementation pilots excluded.
Additional2000 eQTL calibration cohorts:500 per null/abundance-only and size.
48 common variants in12 synthetic LD regions;24 RNA genes in4 synthetic modules.
Primary/test/calibration cohort seeds are disjoint,2400 seeds in total.
Full frozen settings and source hashes: protocol/design.json and source_lock.json.

SOFTWARE
Python scientific packages pinned in requirements.txt.
R4.5.1, mixOmics6.33.0 and jsonlite2.0.0 were used locally.
MOFA uses official mofapy2. DIABLO uses official mixOmics block.splsda.
Quarto renders the shared research manuscript to Word with editable equations.
No cluster or GPU is required; two CPU workers and small panels were used.

ANALYSIS DETAILS
MOFA: unsupervised train-only adjustment/scaling;15% masked-entry factor-budget
selection among4/8 factors; two distinct starts by ELBO; no subtype in factor fitting.
Adjusted logistic factor tests use BH across the nominal factor budget. Top5 RNA
loadings per significant discovery factor nominate genes. Independent factor tests
and direction agreement are reported separately. Train-SD<1e-8 factors are zeroed.
MOFA scores can feed a downstream classifier; DIABLO receives the molecular blocks
directly and does not receive MOFA scores or eQTL-screened inputs.

DIABLO: stratified threefold training validation of1/2 components and3/6 molecular
features per component; clinical block retains6 observed inputs. Final weighted
centroid classes are evaluated on independent participants. No artificial AUC or
probability calibration is computed from DIABLO hard labels.

eQTL: per gene, covariates+subtype+4local genotypes+4genotype-by-subtype interactions.
Omnibus F tests8 local effects; interaction F tests4 interactions while retaining
main effects. BH0.05 separately across24 genes per family. Restricted HC2 wild
bootstrap uses999 shared participant-level Rademacher draws and plus-one P values.
Ordinary-F results and bootstrap sensitivity results remain separately accessible.

TRIANGULATION
MOFA RNA nominations, DIABLO RNA selections and eQTL-associated genes are intersected.
Locus mapping uses the two-gene local region. Module nominations require separate
hypergeometric enrichment/BH across4 groups against the24-gene background. Gene and
module overlap probabilities therefore have different nomination requirements.
No independent biological P value is assigned to overlap from shared measurements.

STABILITY
Selection frequency across20 generated cohorts differs from within-cohort stability.
Five conditional bootstrap fits in each fixed first-replicate fixture retain
selected tuning parameters; MOFA bootstrap fits use one start. Absolute loading
cosines use permutation/sign-invariant matching. Empty-union Jaccard is undefined.

AMENDMENTS AND PRESERVATION
Initial150-iteration MOFA fits are preserved in results/cohorts and results/models.
Only52/200 final fits converged. Uniform1000-cap refits, including tuning/bootstrap
fits, are in results/refined and refined_models;200/200 final fits converged.
The convergence amendment changed no scenario, seed, effect, threshold or factor grid.
Numerically dormant factors were handled in a separate documented finalisation
amendment. Results/final and final_models are the reported primary MOFA results;
original and extended raw fits remain released.
